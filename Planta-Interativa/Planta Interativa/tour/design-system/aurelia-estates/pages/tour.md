# Tour page overrides

These rules override `MASTER.md` for the virtual tour.

## Typography

- Display: **Instrument Serif** 400 for property and room names, prices and the loading title.
- Body and UI: **Manrope** 400–700.
- Brand wordmark: Manrope 600, uppercase, 0.32em tracking.

This replaces MASTER's Cinzel / Josefin Sans, which read as dated against photoreal 3D. Instrument Serif keeps the editorial luxury feel, and Manrope is cleaner at small UI sizes.

## Surfaces

- UI floats over the 3D scene on dark glass panels (`rgba(12,18,18,0.72)`, 14px blur, 1px hairline border, 18px radius).
- Deep teal `#0f766e` stays the primary action colour; muted gold `#d4b67c` marks eyebrows and prices.
- Floor plan: rooms tinted by state (visited: gold, active: teal); glazing drawn as thin teal lines.

## 3D look

- Daylight from a late-afternoon south-west sun; interiors get a warm ~3000K area light.
- Materials: engineered oak, travertine, satin plaster, bronze window frames, brass accents, boucle upholstery.
- Post-processing: ambient occlusion, gentle bloom, PBR Neutral tone mapping and a light vignette.
